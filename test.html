Fuel & Energy Branch Innovating the Future

50 Years of Fuel Engineering

Established at Indian Institute of Technology (ISM) Dhanbad, the Department is one of India's premier centers for technical education and industrial R&D in fuel processing, coal research clean technology integration. The branch expanded its academic and research horizons by incorporating Energy research in 2023.

Fuel & Energy Engineering Vision

Pioneering technical and interdisciplinary research across conventional coal utilization to clean coal technology, biofuels, alternate energy systems, CFD simulations and new generation fuels to advance sustainable resource utilization and global energy security.

Industry-Integrated Rigor

Combining rigorous thermodynamics, reaction kinetics, and process simulation with hands-on industrial projects, characterization labs, and computational modeling.

Research & Testing Infrastructure

Advanced Laboratory Facilities

Hands-on characterization, analytical chemistry, and simulation suites standard in energy engineering research.

Fuel Analysis

Fuel Technology Lab

Core experiments covering Proximate & Ultimate Analysis, Gross Calorific Value (GCV), Free Swelling & Caking Index, Hardgrove Grindability Index (HGI), and characterization of solid/liquid fuels including Flash/Fire point and Cloud/Pour point testing.

Fundamental Fuel Analysis
Thermal Studies

Thermochemical Conversion Lab

Advanced thermal studies including TGA of biomass/char in inert and oxidizing environments, ash fusion temperature analysis, flame velocity/shape characterization for gaseous fuels, and coal plasticity via Giesler Plastometer.

Thermal & Combustion Studies
Liquid Fuel Analysis

Processing of Liquid & Gaseous Fuels Lab

Comprehensive petroleum and gas evaluation involving Reid vapor pressure, Aniline point, Cetane/Octane numbers, ASTM distillation, carbon residue (Conradson/Ramsbottom), Bomb/Junkers calorimetry, and Gas Chromatography.

Petroleum & Gas Analytics
Clean Energy

Energy Technology Lab

State-of-the-art experiments on coke making, char combustion timescales, Techno-Economic Analysis, Life Cycle Assessment (LCA), photovoltaic cell responses, CO2 fixation in solids, and comparative microwave vs. conventional heating.

Clean Energy & Sustainability
Simulation & CFD

CFD of Thermal & Fluid Systems Lab

Comprehensive numerical modeling utilizing FVM to solve 1-D/2-D steady and unsteady diffusion & convection-diffusion systems. Advanced simulations include lid-driven cavity flows, drop tube furnaces, and turbulent premixed flames using ANSYS Fluent and OpenFOAM.

About IIT (ISM) Dhanbad

Founded in 1926 as the Indian School of Mines and converted into an Institute of National Importance under the IIT framework, IIT (ISM) Dhanbad has been at the forefront of natural resource development, energy extraction, and technological advancement for a century.

1. Fuel Testing & Calorimetry Suite
