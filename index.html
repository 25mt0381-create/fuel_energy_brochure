Department of Fuel, Minerals & Metallurgical Engineering

A Century of Energy Engineering

Established at Indian Institute of Technology (ISM) Dhanbad, the Department is one of India's premier centers for technical education and industrial R&D in energy processing, hydrocarbon refining, and clean technology integration.

Fuel & Energy Engineering Vision

The M.Tech program addresses the global dual challenge: ensuring reliable energy security while accelerating net-zero decarbonization. Postgraduates master both conventional fossil fuel processing and next-generation clean tech solutions.

Industry-Integrated Rigor

Combining rigorous thermodynamics, reaction kinetics, and process simulation with hands-on industrial projects, characterization labs, and computational modeling.

About IIT (ISM) Dhanbad

Founded in 1926 as the Indian School of Mines and converted into an Institute of National Importance under the IIT framework, IIT (ISM) Dhanbad has been at the forefront of natural resource development, energy extraction, and technological advancement for a century.

Dept. of Fuel, Minerals & Metallurgical Engineering

Page 04 • Infrastructure R&D Facilities

Advanced Testing & Characterization Labs

1. Fuel Testing & Calorimetry Suite

Bomb Calorimeters, Proximate/Ultimate Analyzers, Flash & Fire Point Testing, Kinematic Viscometers for ASTM-standard liquid and solid fuel characterization.

2. Central Analytical Instrumentation Facility

Gas Chromatography-Mass Spectrometry (GC-MS), Thermogravimetric Analysis (TGA-DSC), FTIR Spectrometer, Powder XRD, and Scanning Electron Microscopy (SEM).

3. Process Simulation & CFD Workstations

High-performance computing cluster running licensed chemical process flowsheeting and fluid mechanics modeling tools.
